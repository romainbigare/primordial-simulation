# The sorted start's probes

For the branch `wip/sorted-start` (the nursery places every world's cover).

- `probe.rs`: rows by latitude of a world at a year (cover, lead kind, kinds alive, dry matter a year, frost gene,
  coldest month). `place.rs`: what the nursery places. Build each as an example of `cli`.
- `rows_seed1.txt`, `rows_seed2.txt`: those rows at years 3, 30, 100, the sorted world against main.
- `slow_ranges.txt`: the key numbers of three seeds on the sorted world.
- `at2.js`, `shoot.sh`, `allshots.sh`: pictures of the page at a year and a zoom, desktop and phone, main | sorted.
- `insp.js`, `insp2.js`, `herb.py`, `cmp.py`, `map.py`: small readers of a square's kinds and of the tables.
- `shots/`: five pairs, main on the left, the sorted world on the right.
