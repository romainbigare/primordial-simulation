# Watch reports

One report per milestone or check point, in simple English (handover 12.4). Pictures go in a
folder with the same name as the report.

- The watch reports for M0, M1, M2 and the M3 check point were shared as separate files and were
  not saved here. From now on every report is saved in this folder.
- `review-m0-m3.md`: the review of Part 1 (M0 to M3), 12 October 2026, with the list of fixes
  done before M4.
- `watch-pre-m4.md`: the fix list done, and three 300-year runs, 13 October 2026. Pictures and
  the full analysis in `watch-pre-m4/`.
- `review-2-before-m4.md`: the second review of Part 1, after the fixes. From reading the code
  only, with the list of 16 items to do before M4 in order, 13 October 2026.
- `watch-2-before-m4.md`: the second fix list done (all 16 items, the new rate law, 7 bugs found by
  the long runs), three 300-year runs with neutral eaters, and the two comparisons (nodes against
  individuals, shared against own brains), 13 October 2026. Pictures, charts and the full analysis
  in `watch-2-before-m4/`.
- `review-3-before-m4.md`: the third review, 13 October 2026: what is still wrong in the math, the
  physics, the chemistry and the world concepts after the second fix list, with the owner's two
  questions (a roster of starting kinds; plants without a brain), a speed profile, and the list of
  15 items to do before M4.
- `watch-3-before-m4.md`: the third fix list done (all 16 items of review 3), three bugs found by
  the long runs and fixed, three 300-year runs at 128 x 128, null runs and roster runs at 64 x 64,
  14 October 2026. Chart, pictures and the full numbers in `watch-3-before-m4/`.
- `watch-4-before-m4.md`: the owner's three changes after report 3 (a child costs what it is built
  of, not its liquid; bodies die of age by their rate of living, with a repair gene; enzymes cost a
  tenth of an eater's upkeep), three 300-year runs, null and roster runs, 14 October 2026.
- `review-4-before-m4.md`: the fourth review, 14 October 2026, from the code and the reports only
  (nothing run): the world is stable and can go to M4 after three small items (a node cannot
  evolve its brain size; the catalyst columns mix the kinds, so the "light gene fell" story is an
  artifact; a member lives about a month, which no column shows), what is still missing for trees
  and for a tool-using kind, and the speed gains in reach.
- `watch-5-before-m4.md`: review 4's list done (brain size can change in nodes; per-kind columns

- `review-5-before-m4.md`: the fifth review, 14 October 2026, on watch report 5's two open
  problems, from the code and the numbers only (nothing run): lives are short because births are
  fast (a member's real life is one over its birth rate, and nothing makes building a body take
  time); the roster did not lose its plants but its small plants (the big kind won by shade; by

- `review-5-before-m4.md`: the fifth review, 14 October 2026: lives are short because births are
