# Decisions taken for the owner

The owner asked the architect to finish the foundation plan on its own: where a decision is needed, the architect
makes a suggestion and takes it as the owner's decision, and lists it here for the owner to review at the end. Each
line: what was decided, why, and what to look at if the owner disagrees. When the owner has reviewed this file, its
kept decisions move to the plan's Section 3 and the file goes.

| # | Phase | Decision | Why | If you disagree |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | E | E4 (the pace of nodes) merged with a known plant-life step error, before the reproduction work | The reproduction rework rewrote the booking where the error sat, and it was waiting for E4 | Fixed since (E11: the pace runs are flat) |
| 2 | E | Selection inside a node (E12) uses one sample member per update, not two; the node's own update is the other side | Two samples cost twice; the side is drawn, so the estimate stays unbiased | `copy/select.rs` |
| 3 | E | E12 skips the sample for a gene the update never reads | It changes nothing in the world (the same to four digits) and saves time | `copy/select.rs` |
| 4 | E | The sample of selection inside a node runs on one update in 2 (`sample_every` 2, a speed setting), and skips the samples whose step cannot reach a threshold in the update (`copy::select::read`): about 7,600 samples a year at 64 × 64 (35% that move nothing) against 14,400 (63%) before the skips | Speed first; each gene still moves at the same mean rate. With the skips, a sample at every update would cost about 5% more than the samples before them (twice the signal), so `sample_every` stays and the samples cost half | At ×2 and ÷2, plant members and life move 2 to 5%, about twice the noise in one seed of two: set `sample_every` to 1 (`params.rs`) |
| 5 | J | The air's one implicit step (J1) lands although Flow is about 1.4 to 1.8 times slower than the old 3-day air | The old air was not converged (rain 3 to 4 times off at short steps); the new one is converged at 12 days against 6-hour steps and gives real land rain (0.88 m a year). Speed comes back by threads for the cell pass, cheaper booking and a second-order step | Keep the old air: revert J1's commit (the A2 references come back) |
| 6 | J | The air steps 12 days, not 15 to 30 | Backward Euler is first order: at 15 days the warmest month is off by more than the noise. 15 to 30 days needs a second-order step (BDF2), left as a later task | `air_hours` in `params.rs` |
| 7 | J | `rain_time` (1 day, tuned) replaces the old explicit limits of the air: with the hour or two real cloud water lives, rain falls on a tenth of the land | The tuned count stays the same (`mix` became anchored) | `docs/numbers.md`, row `rain_time` |
| 8 | F | The check of nodes against individuals (F7) reads years 41 to 50, and 21 to 25 with the roster, not 41-50 and 91-100 as handover 7.1 fixes | At 32 × 32 the eaters as individuals pass 10⁴ bodies by year 70, and the roster's eaters 10⁵ by year 28: too slow for a test | `tape/tests/nodes.rs`; handover 7.1 |
| 9 | E | The heat the bodies' node holds is settled by the survey from per-body rates, not by Flow | Each body keeps its own draw rate, so the same heat reaches each body as before (D6); a per-cell rate would change the physics | `survey.rs`, `Body::keep`, `Body::drew` |
| 10 | E | One even 10-day step for the physics and the air (`phys_days` 10, `air_hours` 240), the air's step booked whole and dividing the physics step | The long-step test holds the climate of 6-hour air and 3-day physics steps within the noise; Change costs half and the booking a physics step at a time goes | `phys_days`, `air_hours` in `params.rs` (a 12-day air step needs the booking through its span back) |
| 11 | E | For a body without a brain the `INTERVAL` gene stays the turnover time of its stores and its frost (`Body::paces`), though it takes no decisions and a shorter one costs it nothing; `laws.md` 4.4 says so | Every other time constant changes the world or breaks the pace of nodes: one level per update ties the refill to `node_days`, a speed setting (the ×2 and ÷2 test); the `LIQ` gene as its rate (plants' about 2 a day) lets them refill about 70 levels per update; a new number would be tuned. The light-eaters' `INTERVAL` mean moved 1.30 to 1.33 in 30 years: the effect is small today | Pick a time constant (a physiological gene with a cost, or a new anchored number) and use it in `Body::paces` for bodies without a brain |
| 12 | K | A world can differ from year 3 between an `ew` built alone and one built with the tests (the test kit is in the build): the compiler folds some math functions with constant arguments differently. Accepted: the same build gives the same history (handover 12.5), and runs are compared only between binaries built the same way | Making it equal would need our own math functions in core for every transcendental function, which is the cross-build determinism handover 14.5 bans | Use `cargo build --release -p cli` for runs to compare |
