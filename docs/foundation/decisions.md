# Decisions taken for the owner

The owner asked the architect to finish the foundation plan on its own: where a decision is needed, the architect
makes a suggestion and takes it as the owner's decision, and lists it here for the owner to review at the end. Each
line: what was decided, why, and what to look at if the owner disagrees; the runs behind each are in
`docs/history.md` and the commit messages. When the owner has reviewed this file, its kept decisions move to the
plan's Section 3 and the file goes.
