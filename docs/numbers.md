# The numbers of the world: what each one is, what it is anchored to, and how far to trust it

**Written:** 14 October 2026 (review 5, row 6; handover 3 and 14.3), marks revised the same day by review 6 (finding 9). **Keep it true:** a test (`core/tests/numbers.rs`) fails when a field of `Physics` or `Config` has no row here, or a row names a field that does not exist. A new number cannot be added without saying what it is.

## How to read this table

Every field of `Config` and of `Physics` (`core/src/params.rs`) has one row. A row says:

- **unit:** the unit of the number.
- **fact:** the fact of matter or energy it is (a capacity, a cost, a conductance, a rate, a reference mass, a speed-tool setting...). A number that is not a fact of matter or energy is a setting, and the row says so.
- **anchor:** the real-world size it is anchored to, with the source in a few words. "Run:" means the run or the reason it was set for. "None found" means I found no real size in the code, the handover or the reports.
- **mark:** one of three, plus two for things that are not numbers.
  - **physical:** a measured size, used as measured.
  - **anchored:** a real order of magnitude, chosen within it.
  - **tuned:** set so a run came out a certain way. The row names the run or the reason. `tuned, first guess` is the same mark for a number with no real size and no recorded run: it was written by hand in M0 to M3 to get a sensible start and kept (commit `bc47a66` moved them into `Physics` unchanged). They count as tuned, as they have no size behind them. The first job of any of them is to find a size and become anchored.
  - **switch:** an on/off setting of an experiment, not a number of the world. **container:** a field that holds other rows.

**The M7 world search varies only tuned and anchored numbers, within their anchors, and never the physical ones.** Switches are not searched. For an anchored number "within its anchor" means within the real order named in its row (about a factor of 3 each way unless the row says more). For a tuned number it means around its value, by the factor its row suggests, and every run prints the numbers it used (`physics.json`, review 5, drift 1, fix 4).

**The budget for tuned numbers is in handover 14.3.** The count is at the end of this file.

Some numbers in this file were found by reading doc comments and reports, not by checking a source. Where I give my own arithmetic for a real size, the row says "my arithmetic". Rows I am less sure of say "unchecked". **Source check of 15 Oct 2026:** the real figures written from memory were looked up. "checked" means a source agrees (author and year given); "checked in part" means the source was found for part of the figure; "differs" means a real figure is more than 2 times off the one used (the number is not changed; the row gives the real range). Only what a page or paper showed counts as checked; the rest keeps "unchecked".

## Real figures the reports compare with (checked 15 Oct 2026)

Not rows of the model; the figures of `docs/reports/review-7-lives.md`, checked against sources.

- **Large plant-eaters, 20 t/km²: differs.** Hempson et al. 2015 (Science 350: 1056) put the reconstructed biomass of African large herbivores at 53 to 11,810 kg/km² over 1° squares, the peak of the typical curve about 1,700 kg/km² (without elephants). Damuth 1981 (Nature 290: 699; from memory): density = 4.23 m^-0.75 per km² for plant-eaters, so about 250 to 450 kg/km² for 50 to 500 kg animals. The model's 240 t/km² is 20 times the highest real square and over 100 times the typical one; "10 times" in the report is too small, as 20 t/km² is the top of the real range.
- **Remaining lives of adults:** bison 8 to 12 y: adult cows survive 0.92 a year (Fuller et al. 2007, J Wildl Manage 71) so 12 y, 0.95 a year in later counts (Yellowstone report 2023), 20 y; agrees. Deer 4 to 7 y: wild adult survival 0.83 to 0.91 a year with no hunting (Wisconsin study, as quoted by secondary pages) so 5 to 11 y; agrees. Mouse 0.3 to 0.5 y: a farm study (eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/6417) found apparent survival 0.54 a month, so 0.13 y if taken at face value (it counts leaving); 2 to 4 times under the table, not settled. Rabbit 1 to 2 y and the herb: no source found. A 7.6 to 9.5 y record exists for a wild rabbit.
- **P/B a year of the table (3 to 5, 1 to 2, 0.3 to 0.5, 0.15 to 0.25, about 1): not checked.** Banse and Mosher 1980 (Ecol. Monogr. 50: 355) and Humphreys 1979 (J Anim Ecol 48: 427) are the sources to read; their tables could not be opened. They fall with size, as the table does.
