# Speed x10: decisions

The decisions of this round, each with its reason and what was not chosen. The owner asked the architect to take them
all and log them. Numbers refer to `plan.md` and the design files in `design/`.

| ID | Step | Decision | Reason | Not chosen |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| X1 | all | The work happens on the branch `speed/x10`. Steps merge into it one at a time; `main` stays as the last round left it until the owner merges the branch | The owner asked for a new branch; the laws change, so `main` keeps the last checked world | Work on `main` |
| X2 | R1 | The core ceiling is 11,500 code lines (`cli/tests/core_size.rs`, `CLAUDE.md` 2.11). The four designs need about +1,250 to +1,600 net with every step built; 11,500 leaves room for that and for the deletions to land later. Each step names what it deletes | Handover 14.3 allows a raise with a reason; the gated and research steps may not all be built | 10,100 (the plants design alone needs most of it); no ceiling |
| X3 | E1, E8 | Owner's idea 4 is a permission with a price: a step may make the history depend on the number of threads only if it gains at least 5% of a year on 4 cores over the best fixed-order design. The pipeline E1 is fixed-order (the order of its writes is fixed by the data), so the golden test and the order test stay equality tests for any number of threads. E8 builds the order-free variant and measures it against E1 | The engine design found the serial share is one thread doing the cross-domain writes, not the cost of determinism; the same gain with the same history is worth more | Drop determinism at once |
| X4 | E6 | Numbers that hold matter or energy stay 64-bit. "Exact books per cell and month" means: each move booked on both sides with one 64-bit number, one writer per number, the books checked each month in the golden test and closed per cell. 32-bit is allowed for numbers that hold nothing (cards, tables, brain rows, faces) where a step measures a gain. E6 builds a 32-bit store on a branch and measures it | The world's amounts span 27 decades (an f32 holds 7 digits, an i64 19 decades); the reactor needs 64 bits; the data is 2 to 5 MB with almost no last-level misses (E1 of the last round), so 32 bits buy little memory speed | 32-bit store on the branch at once |
| X5 | R1 | Check level 3 ("the same world") is the key-number test, the nodes check and the pace tests; a level-3 step re-pins golden in its commit and runs the key-number test once. The light-eaters' stored share (S50) and the sleep bias (S84) are known failures; a step must not move them past their seeds' noise unless it says why | The same rule as the last round, with this round's known failures named | Rebuild the key numbers first |
| X6 | R1 | Plants and animals keep one energy law in the end: P1 builds the month law for plants, P1b gives it to animals. Between P1 and P1b the two laws are written in `docs/laws.md` side by side | One place for each idea (`CLAUDE.md` 2.1); MI6 needs it | Plants only |
| X7 | P1, P3 | A plant is a body of a lineage with no brain (`Life::brainless`). Plants stay `Body` values in their cell's list, as *stands* (a plant node), so faces, taking, cover, shade, sums, light marks, draws, the tape, the viewer and the snapshot change little. P3 then steps them per cell in the survey and takes them out of the ring | Storage layout gave no speed (E1 of the last round); the cost is in the law; this keeps the plants' evolution (the breeder's equation of `copy::select`, mutation, splits, merges, dispersal) | A new field type replacing plant bodies |
| X8 | P1, P5 | The growth table is a card: the constants of a genome's month law, made in `prepare`, which runs at every change of genes, so it is never stale and it is not a cache. A grid table over light, heat, water and stores is built only on a branch for discrete strains (P5) and measured | A grid has 7 or more inputs, costs 18 to 200 million instructions to build, and selection moves genes at every update | Grid table on the branch's main line |
| X9 | P1, P1b | Upkeep and income are paid together over a stand's interval by a stock equation solved exactly per phase (income the least of light, carbon gas and catalyst; growth only above a reserve of `reserve_days` of upkeep; members die at (1 - P/U) / `reserve_days` with an empty stock; Blackman 1905, Thornley 1972, Sitch et al. 2003). It removes C2's splitting error | C2 failed because upkeep was paid before the interval's light; the death rate went with 1/interval | A longer interval with today's law |
| X10 | AIR | The air keeps its 10-day step and its 4 x 4 grid on the benches; AIR1 to AIR3 are built; AIR4 (calm months) only after its research step finds why long steps warm the winters; AIR5 (a coarser grid) is measured on a branch against the climate tests | Measured: 15 days +0.30 K, 30 days +0.65 K against a limit of 0.3 K; the coarse solve is only 6 to 8% of the air step | Longer air steps now; a coarser grid now |
| X11 | MI3, MI4, MI5 | Habits: a body thinks again when one of its senses moved more than `wake` (0.25, one e-fold of a store), a body of another lineage came or left its cell, its habit is no longer possible, or `sleep_most` (90 days) passed; between thoughts it acts by the chances of its last thought and learns at its next thought from the whole habit (Sutton, Precup and Singh 1999). `--full-minds` is the control run. Groups: the senses of a place are shared per survey; a node's movers leave as one group. One decision for unlike nodes is not built | Decisions cost 16% of a year; acting is most of it; one decision for unlike nodes would merge unlike nodes (S66), a world choice | Group decisions over unlike nodes |
| X12 | MI7 | Batched networks are built and measured (decisions first for all due bodies of a domain, acts after: level 3) even though the design expects little (each body owns 76% of its brain's weights); kept only if the network's share falls by half and a year gains at least 2% | The owner asked for it to be tried; the measure decides | Not built |
| X13 | MI8 | A lineage with at least `Config::full_hidden` hidden units is never a node and thinks at every update (the full-mind path); today no lineage reaches it, so it costs nothing until smart bodies come | The three rules for smart bodies: individuals stay real, two kinds of body code | Build smart bodies now |
