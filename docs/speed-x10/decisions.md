# Speed x10: decisions

The decisions of this round, each with its reason and what was not chosen. The owner asked the architect to take them
all, to log them, and to drop bad ideas from the start; a dropped strategy brings an alternative. Step names refer to
`plan.md` (the single source of the steps) and the design files in `design/`. A row changed by a later decision says
so in its text.
