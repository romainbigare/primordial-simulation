2 · Player

Time is the heart of the page, so the bottom is a player: a big date, a bar for the year with its seasons, speed − and +. Three tools sit on the right edge. Inspect uses a fixed cross in the middle: you move the map under it, and the numbers change live. No need to "hover" on a phone.

4 · App with tabs

Feels like a normal phone app. A tab bar at the bottom: Map, Layers, Inspect, Charts, Create. A thin bar at the top holds the date and play. Layers become a grid of tiles, each with a small colour bar, so you see what a layer looks like before you pick it.
